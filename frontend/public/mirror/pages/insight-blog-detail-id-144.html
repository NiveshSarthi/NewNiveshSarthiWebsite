Urban Cubes 71: Gurgaon's Most Sought-After SCO Commercial Destination in Sector 71

Urban Cubes 71 by Whiteland Corporation is redefining commercial real estate in Gurugram — a RERA-approved SCO (Shop-Cum-Office) development at Sector 71 that combines prime location, high-street retail architecture, and outstanding investment potential.

When investors and business owners look for the best SCO plots in Gurgaon, one name consistently emerges at the top: Whiteland Urban Cubes 71. Situated on the prestigious Southern Peripheral Road (SPR) in Sector 71, this landmark project by Whiteland Corporation has quickly established itself as a benchmark for modern commercial development in the Delhi-NCR region.

Whether you are a retail brand seeking a flagship storefront, an F&B entrepreneur eyeing high-footfall zones, or an investor hunting for high-ROI commercial plots in Gurugram — Urban Cubes 71 ticks every box.

What is Urban Cubes 71? An Overview

Urban Cubes 71 is a RERA-approved (Registration No. GGM/489/221/2021/57) commercial development by Whiteland Corporation located in Sector 71, Gurugram. Spread across 2.65 acres, it introduces Gurugram's first SCO concept with horizontal leasing — a distinctive business model that has attracted massive interest from national and international retail players.

The project features 34 contemporary shop-cum-office (SCO) units ranging from 540 to 1,462 sq. ft., each following a Ground + 4 (G+4) configuration. With a 259-metre-wide frontage on the main SPR, maximum development faces the main road — ensuring unmatched visibility and free advertising for all occupants.

Location Advantage: Why Sector 71 SPR is Gurgaon's New Commercial Hub

Location is the cornerstone of any successful commercial investment, and Urban Cubes 71 Sector 71 is strategically positioned at the junction of the 150-metre-wide Golf Course Extension Road and the 84-metre-wide dividing road. This dual-frontage address on the Southern Peripheral Road (SPR) ensures visibility from both directions.

Connectivity Highlights

  • Direct access to NH-8 (National Highway 8), Sohna Road, and Dwarka Expressway
  • 3 minutes from the future Cloverleaf flyover interchange
  • Close proximity to Golf Course Extension Road — one of Gurgaon's premium corridors
  • Adjacent to DLF Alameda, a fully developed premium residential township
  • Near Tatvam Villas and other upscale residential communities

Corporate Neighbourhood — Major MNC Office Proximity

One of the strongest footfall drivers for any commercial project is proximity to large office clusters. Urban Cubes 71 sits in the immediate catchment of leading multinationals including American Express, Google, TCS, and Genpact. This ensures a built-in daily audience of thousands of working professionals visiting retail, dining, and lifestyle spaces in the vicinity.

The project is surrounded by a dense catchment of approximately 70,000 residential units and over 33 million sq. ft. of office space — making organic footfall a near certainty rather than a hope.

Project Features & Amenities: What Sets Urban Cubes 71 Apart

Whiteland Corporation designed Urban Cubes 71 not just as a collection of commercial plots, but as a curated retail and lifestyle ecosystem. Here are the standout features:

  • Double-height retail storefronts for maximum visual impact and branding
  • Central piazza with landscaped open spaces for community engagement
  • Rooftop dining terraces ideal for premium F&B brands
  • Dedicated food truck zones for experiential and pop-up retail
  • Amphitheater with LED screen for events and brand activations
  • Centralized power backup and HVAC solutions across all units
  • Surface + basement parking with ample capacity
  • 24×7 security with modern surveillance systems
  • Water treatment and centralised services infrastructure
  • LEED Platinum certified green building standards
  • Horizontal leasing provision — first of its kind in Gurgaon SCOs

SCO Plots at Urban Cubes 71: Investment Details & Pricing

The SCO plots in Sector 71 Gurgaon at Urban Cubes 71 offer investors ownership of commercial land parcels — a significant advantage over traditional leased commercial spaces. Ownership gives long-term capital appreciation potential in addition to rental income.

Unit sizes range from 540 to 1,462 sq. ft. with G+4 configuration. Pricing starts at approximately ₹3.60 Cr and goes up to ₹9.75 Cr depending on the unit size, floor, and frontage. The project is Ready to Move.

Key Investment Highlights

  • Ready-to-move commercial SCO units — no construction wait
  • Corner plots with dual frontage for greatest visibility
  • Flexible unit sizes suitable for small boutiques to large offices
  • G+4 options with land and porch ownership rights
  • Car parking facilities included with SCO plots
  • Horizontal leasing model offers more flexible occupancy terms
  • RERA-approved project ensures complete legal transparency

Why Urban Cubes 71 is the Best Commercial Investment in Gurgaon 2026

The Gurugram commercial real estate market has been witnessing unprecedented growth, particularly along the Golf Course Extension and Southern Peripheral Road corridors. Whiteland Urban Cubes 71 is ideally positioned to benefit from this macro tailwind.

Areas along Sector 71 have emerged as a new-age micro-market of Gurugram, blending premium residential density with rapid commercial development. Property appreciation in this corridor has consistently outperformed other Gurgaon micro-markets over the past five years — and with the future Cloverleaf flyover set to further improve connectivity, values are poised to rise further.

For retail and F&B brands, the project offers a rare combination: ownership (not lease) of high-street plots with a ready catchment of 70,000+ families, meaning the audience is already there. For investors, the rental yield potential from premium commercial SCOs on SPR remains among the highest in Gurugram.

Should You Invest in Urban Cubes 71?

If you are looking for a premium commercial SCO investment in Gurgaon with strong fundamentals — prime location, established developer, RERA approval, LEED certification, and a ready-to-occupy status — Whiteland Urban Cubes 71 stands in a class of its own on the SPR corridor.

The combination of ownership rights, high-density catchment, corporate proximity, and first-mover advantage on Gurgaon's fastest-growing commercial micro-market makes this an opportunity that discerning investors and business owners should evaluate seriously in 2026.
