Ready to Move Luxury Apartments in Gurgaon 2026: Why Krisumi Waterfall Residences Is a Rare Find Under ₹10 Crore

In Gurgaon's ultra-competitive luxury housing market, finding a ready-to-move apartment with Japanese precision, Tata Projects construction quality, and a starting price under ₹10 crore is not just rare — it is almost impossible. Almost.

Gurgaon's luxury residential market in 2026 is booming — and so are its prices. Property rates in Gurgaon now range from ₹15,000 to ₹20,000 per sq. ft. in premium locations, and the starting price for luxury apartments typically begins at around ₹2.50–₹5 Cr for a 2 BHK and ₹5.65–₹6.5 Cr for 3 BHK units. In this landscape, a ready-to-move project that delivers true luxury — international architecture, premium construction, and a branded identity — at under ₹10 crore is a genuine market anomaly. Krisumi Waterfall Residences in Sector 36A, Gurgaon, is exactly that.

What makes it truly luxury — not just on paper

The word "luxury" has been diluted by overuse in Indian real estate marketing. Krisumi Waterfall Residences earns it on credentials alone. It is India's first Indo-Japanese mega real estate project, designed by Nikken Sekkei — the Japanese architectural firm behind the iconic Tokyo Skytree. Every inch of the development reflects Nikken Sekkei's core philosophy: maximising natural light, creating open breathing spaces, and building a deep visual connection with nature.

The project is backed by two giants: the 400-year-old Japanese conglomerate Sumitomo Corporation and India's Krishna Group, combining Sumitomo's global quality standards with Krishna Group's trusted local execution. Construction was handed to Tata Projects — one of India's most reliable contractors — ensuring that the delivery standard matches the design ambition. The result: a project where the luxury is structural, not cosmetic.

The rare advantage: ready to move, right now

In Gurgaon's 2026 luxury market, buying a ready-to-move asset eliminates execution risk, though it typically commands a ~20% premium over under-construction options. That premium is usually worth paying — because what you see is exactly what you get. No construction delays. No specification changes. No "possession in 2029" uncertainty. Krisumi Waterfall Residences achieved possession in November 2024, meaning residents are already living in it, the community is active, and the clubhouse is fully operational.

This is particularly significant for buyers who have watched other Gurgaon luxury projects repeatedly push back timelines. At Krisumi, the wait is over. The question is simply: which unit.

Under ₹10 crore: what you actually get

A 2 BHK apartment at Krisumi Waterfall Residences starts from ₹3.62 crore, a 3 BHK from ₹6.32 crore onwards, and a 4 BHK from ₹9.17 crore — making the full range of configurations accessible within the ₹10 crore ceiling. Compare this to the broader Gurgaon luxury market, where ready-to-move 4 BHK apartments in comparable addresses regularly cross ₹12–20 crore. The Krisumi pricing reflects a project that was launched in 2019 and has appreciated steadily — early buyers have already seen significant gains, and the current price still represents one of the better entry points available for this tier of luxury product on Dwarka Expressway.

 

Five reasons it stands apart in the RTM luxury segment

  • Only ready-to-move Indo-Japanese luxury project in Gurgaon — no comparable substitute exists in this format
  • Designed by Nikken Sekkei (Tokyo Skytree architect) — design credibility that most Indian luxury projects simply cannot match
  • Built by Tata Projects — construction quality assurance that is as close to guaranteed as Gurgaon's market offers
  • 36,000 sq. ft. clubhouse with spa, gym, restaurant, bar lounge, mini-theatre and sports courts — fully operational, not promised
  • RERA registered (HARERA GGM/2018/03) — complete legal transparency for buyers and investors

Location: Sector 36A and the Dwarka Expressway advantage

Sector 36A is rapidly transforming into a high-demand micro-market with property appreciation of 12–15% YOY and steady rental income of up to 4% annually. The location connects seamlessly to NH-48, IGI Airport (approximately 25 km), Cyber City (18 km), and Udyog Vihar — the daily commute corridors of Gurgaon's largest professional population. With the Delhi-Mumbai Industrial Corridor, Global City Project, and Gurugram Metro extension all in the pipeline for the area, the structural tailwinds for Sector 36A pricing are firmly in place.
