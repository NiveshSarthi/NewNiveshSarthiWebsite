Signature Global Lamborghini Residences Gurgaon – India's First Tonino Lamborghini Branded Homes

Discover Signature Global Lamborghini Residences in Sector 71 Gurgaon. India's first Tonino Lamborghini branded luxury homes — 812 residences, 3 & 4.5 BHK, starting ₹5 Cr on SPR.

Signature Global Lamborghini Residences, Sector 71 Gurgaon: India's First Tonino Lamborghini Branded Luxury Homes

India's ultra-luxury residential market just received its most defining address yet. Signature Global Lamborghini Residences — a landmark collaboration between one of India's fastest-growing developers and the iconic Italian lifestyle brand Tonino Lamborghini — has arrived in Sector 71, Gurugram. And nothing about it is ordinary.

When Pradeep Aggarwal, Founder and Chairman of Signature Global, said "we are not just building apartments; we are creating a lifestyle statement," he was not speaking in marketing language. He was describing a project that genuinely belongs in a different category from anything Gurgaon — or India — has seen before in residential real estate.

Announced on April 16, 2026, Signature Global Lamborghini Residences — officially named Tonino Lamborghini Residences Gurugram — marks the Italian brand's first-ever entry into Indian residential real estate. Set across 12.40 acres on the Southern Peripheral Road in Sector 71, this ₹2,890 crore development will deliver 812 ultra-exclusive residences across five high-rise towers, each sculpted in the unmistakable design language of the Lamborghini legacy.

The legend behind the residences: who is Tonino Lamborghini?

Tonino Lamborghini is the son of Ferruccio Lamborghini — the legendary Italian engineer and visionary who founded Automobili Lamborghini in 1963. While the automotive brand grew into one of the world's most coveted supercar manufacturers, Tonino Lamborghini carved his own identity as a luxury lifestyle empire, extending the Lamborghini design philosophy into fashion, accessories, hospitality, and now — for the first time in India — branded residential real estate.

Tonino Lamborghini S.p.A. operates through its Total Living division, which translates the brand's core DNA — bold aerodynamic forms, mechanical precision, Italian craftsmanship, and an unapologetic assertion of status — into architectural and interior environments. Projects bearing the Tonino Lamborghini name have been developed across Europe, the Middle East, and Asia, each one a collaboration between the brand's design team and a local premium developer.

India is now on that map. And Gurugram — specifically Sector 71 — is the address Tonino Lamborghini has chosen for its Indian debut.

"Every project bearing my name goes beyond architecture — it represents a way of living, a personal idea of contemporary luxury rooted in craftsmanship, innovation, and my character. India has always fascinated me for its profound history and cultural richness."

Architecture & design: Lamborghini DNA in every facade

The Signature Global Lamborghini Residences are not a branding exercise — they are an architectural expression of a legacy. The design direction, led by Angela Krieger of Tonino Lamborghini Total Living, draws directly from the brand's automotive heritage: aerodynamically inspired tower silhouettes, bold angular facades, mechanical-aesthetic detailing, and a material palette anchored in carbon-fibre finishes, Italian-themed cabinetry, and floor-to-ceiling glazing that floods every residence with light and city views.

The architectural lineage of the Lamborghini supercar — the aggressive bodywork, the precision engineering, the command presence — is translated into a residential idiom that feels simultaneously futuristic and timeless. Five towers rise to approximately G+40 floors, each a sculptural statement on the Sector 71 skyline. The low-density layout across 12.4 acres ensures that open green spaces, Miyawaki forests, and sky gardens surround the towers rather than being compressed between them.

Interior design highlights

  • Carbon-fibre inspired finishes and bespoke Italian cabinetry throughout
  • Floor-to-ceiling windows with panoramic city and green views
  • Double-height grand entrance lobby with signature Lamborghini design elements
  • Expansive balconies designed for seamless indoor-outdoor living
  • High ceiling residences with premium acoustic and thermal treatment
  • Curated art installations and bespoke lighting concepts in common areas
  • Lamborghini-themed lounge, designer beverage café, and fine-dining areas in the clubhouse

Amenities: a five-star lifestyle, every single day

Ultra-luxury is a word that is overused in Indian real estate. At Signature Global Lamborghini Residences, it is earned. The amenity ecosystem here is not a checklist — it is a curated lifestyle universe designed around the concept of "inner balance" through a sensorial approach to well-being, as described in Tonino Lamborghini's own brief for the project.

Wellness & fitness

  • Infinity-edge swimming pool with resort-quality landscaping
  • State-of-the-art gymnasium with professional fitness equipment
  • Dedicated spa, sauna, and salon with premium wellness treatments
  • Yoga and meditation zones designed for sensorial well-being
  • Jogging and walking trails within a Miyawaki urban forest setting

Social & leisure

  • Grand multi-floor clubhouse with Lamborghini-themed lounge zones
  • Fine-dining restaurant and designer beverage café
  • Business lounge and co-working facilities
  • Sports facilities including courts and outdoor recreation zones
  • Sky gardens and rooftop leisure decks with panoramic views
  • Dedicated children's play areas and family leisure spaces

Security & infrastructure

  • 5-tier security system with 24×7 CCTV surveillance and concierge
  • High-speed elevators for smooth vertical movement across 40 floors
  • Centralized power backup, advanced HVAC systems
  • Ample basement and surface parking with EV charging provisions
  • Smart home features and building automation systems

The branded residences segment is growing at a 19% year-on-year rate globally. India is currently ranked 6th worldwide in branded residence supply — and Gurugram leads the country in this segment. Signature Global Lamborghini Residences arrives at precisely the moment when Indian HNI buyers are actively seeking globally benchmarked residential experiences in their home city.

Location: why Sector 71, Southern Peripheral Road is the right address

Even without the Lamborghini name, a project on Sector 71 SPR would be considered prime in the context of Gurgaon's luxury residential market. With it, the address becomes a defining statement. The Southern Peripheral Road has emerged as Gurgaon's fastest-growing luxury corridor over the past five years — combining premium residential density, MNC office clusters, and infrastructure upgrades that are transforming the belt between Golf Course Extension Road and Sohna Road into the city's most dynamic address.

The SPR corridor currently has among the highest residential price appreciation rates in all of Gurugram, driven by limited premium supply, improving metro connectivity, and the planned Cloverleaf interchange. For buyers of Signature Global Lamborghini Residences, this means that the investment case extends well beyond the lifestyle value of the Lamborghini brand.

The investment case: beyond the badge

Branded residences consistently command a 20–30% price premium over comparable non-branded luxury properties in the same micro-market. This premium reflects not just the brand value, but the rigorous design standards, managed maintenance, and exclusivity that branded developments enforce — all of which protect and enhance long-term asset value.

Why branded residences hold their value

  • Global brand recognition creates demand from NRI and international buyers — widening the resale audience
  • Low density (812 units across 12.4 acres) ensures scarcity, which is the single strongest driver of price appreciation
  • Tonino Lamborghini's commitment to lifelong maintenance of all clubhouse, facade, and common areas guarantees quality preservation
  • SPR corridor appreciation has been consistently above Gurgaon average over the past five years
  • ₹4,000 crore GDV with India's luxury housing market growing rapidly — early buyers typically see the highest gains
  • Signature Global's 13% Delhi-NCR and 27% Gurgaon market share provides developer execution confidence

Signature Global's track record

Signature Global has delivered over 11 million sq. ft. of residential area and currently has 16.4 million sq. ft. of ongoing projects with a further 29.6 million sq. ft. in forthcoming developments. The company commands a 27% market share in Gurgaon — a figure that reflects not just scale but the trust Indian homebuyers place in its execution capability. This is not a developer entering luxury for the first time as a vanity exercise. It is one of the NCR's most powerful real estate companies making a deliberate, well-resourced push into the ultra-luxury segment.

The final word

In a city that has seen countless luxury residential launches, Signature Global Lamborghini Residences occupies a category that did not previously exist in India. It is not a luxury project with premium amenities. It is a globally branded residential masterpiece — designed, curated, and maintained by one of the world's most recognisable Italian luxury names — planted at the most strategically compelling address in Gurugram's fastest-growing corridor.

For buyers who measure home not by square footage but by the statement it makes to the world, there is no more powerful address available in India today. For investors who understand that branded scarcity plus SPR appreciation is a rare combination, the entry point of ₹5 crore into a project with a ₹4,000 crore GDV is one that will look very different in 2033.

The legend has arrived. And its address is Sector 71, Gurugram.
