Property Description

About the project
Godrej Connaught One is one of the most prestigious ultra-luxury residential developments in India, located in the heart of Connaught Place. Developed by Godrej Properties, this project offers a rare opportunity to own a premium residence in Delhi’s most iconic and centrally located area.

📍 Prime Location Advantage

The biggest highlight of this project is its prime location in Connaught Place (CP)—often referred to as the commercial and cultural heart of Delhi. The address itself carries immense prestige, as CP is surrounded by key landmarks such as:

India Gate
Rashtrapati Bhavan
Parliament House

The project is situated near Bangla Sahib Road, offering excellent connectivity to all parts of Delhi via road, metro, and public transport. The Rajiv Chowk Metro Station is just minutes away, ensuring seamless connectivity across NCR.

Living here means being at the center of everything—business hubs, luxury hotels, shopping destinations, and fine dining—all within close proximity.

🏡 Project Overview & Design

Spread across approximately 1 acre, Godrej Connaught One is a low-density luxury development featuring just 46 exclusive residences in a single iconic tower.

The project offers:

3 BHK and 4 BHK luxury apartments
Sizes ranging from ~2200 sq. ft. to 3100 sq. ft.

This limited inventory ensures maximum privacy, exclusivity, and spacious living, which is extremely rare in central Delhi.

The design blends modern architecture with the classic charm of Lutyens’ Delhi, creating a timeless and elegant living experience.

🌟 Ultra-Luxury Lifestyle & Amenities

Godrej Connaught One is positioned as a “7-star living experience”, offering world-class amenities such as:

Temperature-controlled swimming pool
Luxury spa & salon
High-end gymnasium
Concierge services
Private party decks and lounges

The project is designed to deliver a hotel-like lifestyle, catering to HNIs, business leaders, and diplomats.

🏗️ Architecture & Features

The project emphasizes:

High ceilings and large living spaces
Premium interiors and finishes
Landscaped green areas
Advanced security systems

It also includes a grand entrance lobby (approx. 20 ft high), enhancing the luxury feel from the moment you enter.

The low-rise (G+8 approx.) structure ensures privacy and exclusivity, unlike crowded high-rise developments.

💰 Price & Investment Potential

Godrej Connaught One falls in the ultra-luxury segment, with prices typically ranging from:

₹15–16 crore onwards (3 BHK)
Up to ₹25 crore+ (4 BHK) depending on size and configuration
Why Investment is Strong:
Extremely limited supply in Connaught Place
High demand from elite buyers & NRIs
Strong capital appreciation potential
Premium rental value due to location

Central Delhi rarely sees new residential launches, making this project a once-in-a-generation opportunity.

🏢 Developer Profile

The project is developed by Godrej Properties, one of India’s most trusted and reputed real estate developers.

About the Developer:
Part of the Godrej Group (125+ years legacy)
Known for premium residential & commercial projects
Strong track record of quality and timely delivery

The project is being developed under Godrej Projects North LLP and is RERA registered (DLRERA2022P0001), ensuring transparency.

Godrej Connaught One is not just a residential project—it is a status symbol and legacy investment. With its unmatched location, ultra-luxury positioning, and limited inventory, it stands among the most exclusive residential offerings in India.
