Property Description

About the project
M3M Antalya Hills is an exclusive low-rise luxury residential development located in Sector 79, Gurgaon. Nestled at the foothills of the Aravalli range, this premium project by M3M India offers a unique blend of modern architecture, serene surroundings, and high-end lifestyle amenities. Designed for those who seek both comfort and exclusivity, M3M Antalya Hills brings a refreshing concept of independent floor living in one of Gurgaon’s fastest-developing locations.

Project Overview

Spread across a vast expanse of lush green land, M3M Antalya Hills is thoughtfully planned to provide residents with a peaceful and spacious living environment. Unlike high-rise apartments, this project features low-rise independent floors that ensure privacy, better ventilation, and a sense of openness. The project offers well-designed 2.5 BHK and 3.5 BHK residences with modern layouts, making them ideal for families, professionals, and investors alike.

Each unit is crafted with attention to detail, offering spacious living areas, large balconies, and private terraces. The homes are designed to maximize natural light and airflow, creating a healthy and comfortable living space.

Strategic Location Advantage

One of the biggest highlights of M3M Antalya Hills is its prime location in Sector 79, Gurgaon. Situated along the Southern Peripheral Road (SPR), the project enjoys seamless connectivity to major parts of Gurgaon and Delhi NCR.

Residents benefit from easy access to:

NH-8 (Delhi-Jaipur Highway)
Dwarka Expressway
Golf Course Extension Road
Cyber City and Udyog Vihar
Indira Gandhi International (IGI) Airport

Additionally, the project is surrounded by reputed schools, hospitals, shopping malls, and entertainment hubs, making daily life convenient and hassle-free. The proximity to Aravalli Hills also adds a natural charm, offering cleaner air and scenic views.

Modern Architecture & Design

M3M Antalya Hills stands out with its contemporary design and premium construction quality. The low-rise floors are designed to offer a villa-like experience with the convenience of apartment living. Each residence comes with:

Private basement or stilt parking
Wide balconies and terraces
Premium flooring and fittings
Modular kitchen with modern appliances
VRV/VRF air-conditioning system
Smart home automation features

The layout ensures maximum space utilization while maintaining elegance and functionality. The project reflects a perfect balance between aesthetics and practicality.

World-Class Amenities

M3M Antalya Hills offers a wide range of lifestyle amenities that cater to all age groups. The project is designed as a self-sufficient community where residents can enjoy leisure, fitness, and social activities without stepping out.

Key amenities include:

Fully equipped clubhouse
Swimming pool and kids’ pool
Gymnasium and wellness center
Jogging and cycling tracks
Landscaped gardens and open green spaces
Children’s play area
Sports facilities like badminton and basketball courts
24/7 security with CCTV surveillance

These amenities enhance the overall living experience and provide a holistic lifestyle within the community.

Nature-Inspired Living

One of the unique aspects of M3M Antalya Hills is its close proximity to the Aravalli Hills. The project is designed to integrate nature into everyday living. Residents can enjoy fresh air, scenic views, and a peaceful environment away from the hustle and bustle of the city.

The abundance of green spaces, tree-lined pathways, and landscaped gardens creates a refreshing atmosphere that promotes a healthy lifestyle. It’s an ideal choice for those who value tranquility without compromising on modern conveniences.

Investment Potential

Sector 79 Gurgaon is emerging as a prime real estate destination due to its strategic location and rapid infrastructure development. With projects like Dwarka Expressway nearing completion and continuous improvements in road connectivity, property values in this region are expected to appreciate significantly.

M3M Antalya Hills presents a great investment opportunity for both end-users and investors. The low-rise independent floor concept is gaining popularity due to its exclusivity and limited density, which further enhances its value proposition.

Additionally, the reputation of M3M India as a trusted developer adds credibility and assurance of quality construction and timely delivery.

Why Choose M3M Antalya Hills?

There are several reasons why M3M Antalya Hills stands out as a preferred residential choice in Gurgaon:

Low-rise independent floors offering privacy and exclusivity
Prime location with excellent connectivity
Proximity to Aravalli Hills for a peaceful environment
Premium specifications and modern interiors
Wide range of world-class amenities
High potential for future appreciation
Developed by a reputed real estate brand

This project is ideal for those looking to upgrade their lifestyle or invest in a premium property in Gurgaon.

Conclusion

M3M Antalya Hills Gurgaon is more than just a residential project—it’s a lifestyle destination that combines luxury, comfort, and nature. With its strategic location, modern design, and premium amenities, it offers a perfect living experience for families and professionals alike.

Whether you are looking for your dream home or a smart investment, M3M Antalya Hills provides the ideal opportunity to be part of a growing and vibrant community. Its unique low-rise concept, coupled with the beauty of the Aravalli Hills, makes it one of the most desirable residential projects in Gurgaon today.
